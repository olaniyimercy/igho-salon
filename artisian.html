BEHIND THE CRAFT

With over 12 years of professional experience, Igho Godwin brings together technical skill, creativity and a genuine passion for Men's hairdressing.

His approach is rooted in precision understanding individual hair needs and delivering haircuts that compliment each client personal style.
Today, his focus is on independent Men's hairdressing with an emphasis on organic and vegan haircare, offering a more conscious approach to looking after your hair.

MY PHILOSOPHY

I believe haircare should respect both the hair and the environment. My philosophy combines professional craftsmanship with organic and vegan haircare product, choosing thoughtful solutions that support the health and appearance of the hair.

Less unnecessary intervention. More thoughtful care. Better hairdressing & babering.
Natural, Organic and vegan - the foundation of my approach.
